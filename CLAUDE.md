# Writing style for this course's content

Use simple sentence structure, not complex. Write for undergraduate students. Use simple terms. Avoid em-dashes.

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Avoid nested clauses, semicolons, and long chains of subordinate clauses.
- Avoid needless hedging, filler phrases, and formal transition words (e.g. "furthermore," "it is worth noting that").
- Complex sentence structure reads as AI-generated. Simple, direct sentences don't.

Use simple words, not complex ones. Avoid cutthroat sales-war framing and corporate/business jargon (e.g. "leverage," "synergy," "win the battle for market share," "crush the competition"). Plain, everyday words only.

This applies to course content (week pages, `content.md` files, quizzes) — not necessarily to code comments or commit messages.

# Quiz rules

Quizzes must come after the topic they test has been introduced in the content.

Only test information students have already learned. Never test something they haven't been taught yet.
