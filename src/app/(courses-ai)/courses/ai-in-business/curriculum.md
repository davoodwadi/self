# Applications of AI in Business - Course Curriculum

**Subtitle**: Understanding how modern intelligent systems create value, change decisions, and require human leadership  
**Course Level**: Master's  
**Program**: MBA (BUSI 654)  
**Duration**: 10 Weeks  
**Instructor**: Davood Wadi, PhD  
**Summary**: Hands-on course covering machine learning, deep learning for unstructured data, large language models, agentic workflows, and how to evaluate and deploy AI across business functions, strategy, governance, and sustainability.  
**Overview**: This course examines how modern artificial intelligence systems create business value, reshape operational and strategic decisions, and require informed human leadership. Students move from the technical foundations of machine learning, deep learning, large language models, and autonomous agents to concrete applications across marketing, finance, human resources, operations, and product development, concluding with competitive strategy, Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Indigeneity (EDII), and environmental sustainability.

---

## Weekly Breakdown

### Week 1: Foundations of AI and Machine Learning in Business
**Summary**: Framing business decisions as prediction problems, predictive versus generative AI, and deep learning for unstructured data.

- Business operations as repeated decisions made under uncertainty.
- Rule-based software versus statistical learning from historical data.
- Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning in enterprise contexts.
- Deep learning and neural networks for unstructured text, images, and audio.
- Predictive AI versus generative AI and where each creates business value.

### Week 2: Large Language Models, Prompting, and Agentic AI
**Summary**: Foundation models, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, tool use, and autonomous multi-agent workflows.

- How large language models represent and generate text through next-token prediction.
- Prompt engineering, structured outputs, and few-shot task specification.
- Grounding models in enterprise data with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
- From conversational copilots to autonomous agents that plan, call tools, and execute multi-step workflows.
- Governance-first design and human-in-the-loop checkpoints for agentic systems.

### Week 3: AI in Marketing and Consumer Behavior
**Summary**: Demand sensing, dynamic personalization, recommender systems, and AI shopping agents acting on consumers' behalf.

- Customer segmentation, propensity modeling, and churn prediction.
- Recommender systems, search ranking algorithms, and dynamic personalization at scale.
- Generative AI in creative production, copywriting, and marketing research.
- The AI agent as a surrogate consumer that searches, evaluates, and purchases on a shopper's behalf.
- Evaluating model biases and position effects in agent-mediated marketplaces.

### Week 4: AI in Finance
**Summary**: Credit scoring, algorithmic risk management, financial forecasting, and automated fraud detection.

- Credit risk modeling, underwriting automation, and alternative credit data.
- Real-time anomaly detection and fraud prevention in payment networks.
- Algorithmic trading, portfolio optimization, and market sentiment analysis from unstructured filings.
- Explainability, adverse action notices, and regulatory compliance in financial models.
- Stress-testing financial AI systems under regime shifts and tail events.

### Week 5: AI in Human Resources
**Summary**: Talent acquisition, resume screening audits, workforce planning, skill augmentation, and organizational analytics.

- Automated resume screening, candidate matching, and structured interview analysis.
- Workforce analytics, attrition modeling, and internal mobility forecasting.
- Job redesign: separating task automation from human skill augmentation.
- Auditing hiring and performance evaluation algorithms for disparate impact.
- Employee surveillance boundaries, trust, and change management during AI adoption.

### Week 6: AI in Operations and Supply Chain
**Summary**: Demand forecasting, inventory optimization, logistics routing, predictive maintenance, and supply chain visibility.

- Multi-horizon demand forecasting and mitigating the bullwhip effect.
- Inventory optimization, automated replenishment, and warehouse robotics.
- Dynamic vehicle routing, last-mile delivery, and disruption risk sensing.
- Computer vision and sensor fusion for predictive maintenance and quality control.
- Autonomous procurement agents and human oversight in supply chain execution.

### Week 7: AI in Product Development and Software Engineering
**Summary**: Rapid prototyping, vibe coding, agentic software engineering workflows, and AI-assisted product discovery.

- AI-assisted product discovery, customer feedback synthesis, and concept generation.
- Vibe coding and natural-language software creation for rapid functional prototyping.
- Agentic software engineering: code generation, automated testing, and continuous refactoring.
- Managing technical debt, security vulnerabilities, and verification when code is machine-generated.
- How low-barrier software creation shifts responsibilities between product, business units, and IT.

### Week 8: AI in Business Strategy
**Summary**: Corporate strategy, competitive advantage, data moats, build-versus-buy decisions, and evaluating AI return on investment.

- Where durable value accrues in the AI stack: compute, foundation models, data, and application workflows.
- Data moats, proprietary feedback loops, and network effects versus commoditized model APIs.
- Build, fine-tune, or buy: architectural and economic trade-offs for enterprises.
- Designing controlled experiments and A/B tests to measure AI tool quality and business ROI.
- Organizational transformation and leadership required to move from pilots to production.

### Week 9: EDII, Fairness, and Responsible AI
**Summary**: Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Indigeneity in AI systems, algorithmic bias, behavioral auditing, and governance.

- Historical, representation, and measurement bias in enterprise training data.
- Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Indigeneity (EDII) in AI design, deployment, and impact assessment.
- Indigenous data sovereignty, OCAP principles, and community consent.
- Behavioral auditing of large language models for demographic and cultural stereotyping.
- Accountability frameworks, red-teaming, and regulatory compliance across jurisdictions.

### Week 10: Sustainability and the Future of AI
**Summary**: Environmental footprint of compute, energy and water trade-offs, green AI practices, and long-term organizational readiness.

- The environmental cost of training and inference: electricity demand, carbon intensity, and water consumption.
- Hardware lifecycles, embodied emissions, and supply chain pressures for AI infrastructure.
- Green AI practices: right-sizing models, quantization, caching, and carbon-aware scheduling.
- AI applications for climate modeling, grid optimization, and corporate decarbonization.
- Synthesizing technical capability, strategic value, ethical governance, and environmental stewardship.
