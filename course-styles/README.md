# Course styles in reserve

Styles approved for future courses, not yet used by any course. Each has a written spec (`<name>.md`) and a standalone preview (`<name>.preview.html`, open it in a browser) showing the Week 1 Responsible AI slide "Why market algorithms require governance" with its plate drawn in that style.

When a new course adopts one, copy the spec into that course's folder `CLAUDE.md`, build its palette in the course's `globals.css` and `_visuals/kit.tsx`, and remove it from this list. Every course keeps a style of its own, so a style here is used by one course only.

| Style | Spec | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Official Journal | [official-journal.md](official-journal.md) | law, policy, regulation, ethics, public administration |
| Technical standard | [technical-standard.md](technical-standard.md) | operations, risk, safety, compliance, quality management |

[digital-marketing-candidates.preview.html](digital-marketing-candidates.preview.html) compares four new candidates for Digital Marketing and E-Commerce (Mail-Order Catalogue, Shipping and Packaging, Risograph, Interface Wireframe) with the two reserve styles, all drawing the same ranked-listings plate. Mail-Order Catalogue (amber accent, purple contrast, neutral page) was adopted by Digital Marketing & E-Commerce (`src/app/(courses-dme)/courses/digital-marketing-ecommerce/CLAUDE.md`); the other three new candidates were not taken up.

The Patent drawing style from the same round is in use by Responsible AI & Algorithmic Governance (`src/app/(courses-rai)/courses/responsible-ai-governance/CLAUDE.md`).
